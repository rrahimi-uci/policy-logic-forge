\section{Results available in the snapshot}

\subsection{What is measured}

The retained fixture evaluator reports \RuleRecallMatched{} of \RuleRecallGold{}
rules matched for rule recall and \DependencyMatched{} of \DependencyGold{}
dependency edges for the dependency audit. Both artifacts are explicitly
`fixture-only' and use descriptive Wilson intervals with no
population claim. They validate schema, denominator, and refusal behavior;
they are too small to support a benchmark result.

The bounded lowering fixture kills \MutationKilled{} of \MutationTotal{} seeded
mutations across \MutationCases{} cases. Its status is also `fixture-only': it
tests that expected projections and mutations are detected, not that a corpus
of natural-language rules is lowered correctly. The instrument bundle remains
\GThreeStatus{}, so RQ3 has no observed correlation in this snapshot.

The Dutch replay artifact compares \ReplayRows{} rows: \ReplayExactRows{} are exact
and \ReplayMismatchRows{} differ. The mismatch is retained as
`mismatch-reported'. Until it is root-caused and
replayed from pinned inputs, no headline OE anchor is reported. This is a
positive result about observability---the system exposed a disagreement that a
single aggregate score would hide---and a negative result about readiness.

\subsection{What is not measured}

The IR-2 construct census, independent-engine comparison, human AFS frame,
reviewer agreement, and preregistered G3 instrument study are unrun or blocked.
The CEGIR and reward-training extensions are optional and do not support a
claim in this paper. Table \ref{tab:evidence-status} keeps these boundaries
visible.
