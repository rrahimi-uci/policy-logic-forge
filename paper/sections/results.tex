\section{Retained audit results}

\subsection{Reproducibility audit of the Dutch anchor}

The retained anchor audit compares \ReplayRows{} released aggregate rows with a
replay from the declared source. Only \ReplayExactRows{}
(\ReplayExactPercent{}\%) are exact; \ReplayMismatchRows{}
(\ReplayMismatchPercent{}\%) differ. The disagreement is systematic rather than
a single formatting discrepancy: outcome-agree, outcome-disagree, and
outcome-agreement fields each differ on \ReplayOutcomeAgreementMismatches{}
rows, while the declared test count and testability fields each differ on
\ReplayOutcomeTestsMismatches{} rows. Released and replayed files have different
retained hashes.

The audit status is therefore \state{evidencered}{MISMATCH REPORTED}. We do not
choose either aggregate as the OE score, average them, or treat their difference
as model variance. The claimable result is narrower: the released anchor is not
reproducible under the retained replay contract and cannot support downstream
instrument calibration until the discrepancy is root-caused. This is negative
evidence about the anchor and positive evidence that the protocol detects
metric drift.

\subsection{Operational stress run}

The privacy-policy run processed \PrivacyFiles{} files in \PrivacyChunks{}
chunks, completed \PrivacyBatches{} batches, dropped no input bytes, and
retained \PrivacyRules{} rules. Before grounding, \PrivacyReady{}
(\PrivacyReadyPercent{}\%) were marked ready and \PrivacyReview{}
(\PrivacyReviewPercent{}\%) required review. Grounding certified
\PrivacyCertified{} (\PrivacyCertifiedPercent{}\%) and failed \PrivacyFailed{}
(\PrivacyFailedPercent{}\%), with all claimed spans receiving a response. The
retained graph output contains \PrivacyDags{} DAGs, \PrivacyMultiRuleDags{} of
them multi-rule, and one condensed cycle group.

These counts demonstrate complete operational accounting and a substantial
review queue. They do not reveal whether failed grounding is caused by the
source, extraction, evidence threshold, or model behavior, and they are not
precision, recall, or legal-correctness estimates. The run remains
\state{evidenceamber}{EXPLORATORY} because its source bundle is restricted and
it has no independent labels.

\subsection{Contract fixtures and missing scientific anchors}

The rule-recall fixture matches \RuleRecallMatched{}/\RuleRecallGold{} rules;
the dependency fixture matches \DependencyMatched{}/\DependencyGold{} edges;
and the lowering fixture kills \MutationKilled{}/\MutationTotal{} seeded
mutations across \MutationCases{} cases. These provider-free checks validate
schemas, denominators, and expected failure behavior. Their status is
\state{evidencegray}{FIXTURE ONLY}; sample size and construction preclude a
benchmark claim.
