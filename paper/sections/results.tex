\section{Results available in the snapshot}

\subsection{What is measured}

The retained fixture evaluator reports two of three rules matched for rule
recall and one of two dependency edges for the dependency audit. Both artifacts
are explicitly `fixture-only' and use descriptive Wilson intervals with no
population claim. They validate schema, denominator, and refusal behavior;
they are too small to support a benchmark result.

The Dutch replay artifact compares 1,900 rows: 1,108 are exact and 792 differ.
The mismatch is retained as `mismatch-reported'. Until it is root-caused and
replayed from pinned inputs, no headline OE anchor is reported. This is a
positive result about observability---the system exposed a disagreement that a
single aggregate score would hide---and a negative result about readiness.

\subsection{What is not measured}

The IR-2 construct census, independent-engine comparison, human AFS frame,
reviewer agreement, and preregistered G3 instrument study are unrun or blocked.
The CEGIR and reward-training extensions are optional and do not support a
claim in this paper. Table \ref{tab:evidence-status} keeps these boundaries
visible.
