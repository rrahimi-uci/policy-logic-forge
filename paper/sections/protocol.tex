\section{Evaluation protocol}

We define three research questions. \textbf{RQ1} asks which source-bearing rules
execute, refuse, or fail, and why. \textbf{RQ2} asks how faithfully the supported
subset preserves independently annotated source semantics. \textbf{RQ3} asks
whether compiled outcomes agree with gold artifacts and a pinned independent
engine, and whether AFS predicts OE after conditioning on document, model, and
run. A null association, low executable yield, or failed replay is a valid
outcome.

\subsection{Units, anchors, and estimands}

The primary unit is a source-bearing rule, not a model call or generated
sentence. The AFS frame is stratified by document and construct and contains
explicit negatives, exceptions, thresholds, dependencies, and two independent
annotations followed by adjudication. OE uses a frozen input universe and
paired traces from gold, generated, and independently executed models. OPS uses
a complete run manifest; retry attempts and refused cases are retained.

The Dutch decision-model resource is the primary executable anchor because it
pairs source text with production DMN models \citep{graus2026legaltext}.
ContractNLI is a transfer setting for evidence identification rather than an
assumed formal oracle: its labels include pragmatic legal judgments and its
task is document-level inference with cited spans
\citep{koreeda2021contractnli,wang2026limits}. Privacy-policy data is an OPS
stress setting only unless independently annotated.

\subsection{Controls and uncertainty}

Prompts, models, temperature, retry limits, corpus version, and schema are
frozen before evaluation. Source-only, generated-only, gold-artifact, and
independent-engine conditions isolate extraction from execution. Artifact-free
signals are computed without access to gold outputs; leakage canaries and
permuted controls test this isolation. A changed schema or evidence-packet
semantics creates a new run rather than overwriting a checkpoint.

For proportions we report realized numerators and denominators with Wilson
intervals. Document-level comparisons use paired bootstrap intervals;
annotator agreement is reported before and after adjudication. Multiple
comparisons are controlled within preregistered families. We report both micro
and document-macro summaries where unit size varies, and never compute a
headline interval from fixture-only data.

\subsection{Promotion and stop rules}

A result is promotable only when its manifest, hashes, denominator, exclusion
log, protocol version, and required anchor are present. Missing provenance,
gold leakage, an unresolved replay mismatch, or independent-engine drift marks
the affected result \emph{invalid} or \emph{blocked}. The artifact retains the
observation and reason; it does not silently repair or drop the row.
