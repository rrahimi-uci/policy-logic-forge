\section{From implementation to a defensible result}

The remaining work is ordered by claim dependency. First, audit the released
inputs and root-cause the Dutch replay mismatch. Second, obtain licensed
documents and complete the IR-2 census and independent annotations for PIPE-2B
and PIPE-4. Third, pin an external DMN engine and run the source-only and
generated-model controls. Fourth, preregister the G3 instrument study and
collect reviewer outcomes through the workbench. Each gate has a stop rule:
missing provenance, unresolved semantic disagreement, or a changed schema
invalidates the affected checkpoint rather than being silently repaired.

The paper should be resubmitted only after the measurement register is updated
with denominators, confidence intervals, exclusion reasons, and the complete
artifact bundle. If any gate remains blocked by licensing or access, the
appropriate outcome is a transparent audit or negative result, not a proxy
claim. This policy is part of the protocol and can be reviewed before labels
are unsealed.
