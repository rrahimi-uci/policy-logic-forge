\section{Conclusion}

Executable validity is not source fidelity. We introduced an evidence-gated
evaluation contract that makes this distinction operational through separate
AFS, OPS, and OE planes, explicit denominators, and retained epistemic states.
\lexec{} implements the contract as a bounded, provenance-preserving compiler
and review instrument. Its current artifact exposes a consequential negative
result: 792 of 1,900 rows in a benchmark replay disagree, so the anchor is
quarantined rather than converted into a favorable score. A second stress run
demonstrates operational observability while remaining non-claiming about
semantic accuracy. The broader scientific hypothesis---that source-grounded
signals predict correct executable behavior---now has a precise, reproducible
test, but awaits independent labels and execution. Preserving that boundary is
the paper's central result and the condition for any stronger future claim.
