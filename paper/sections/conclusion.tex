\section{Conclusion}

We presented LEXEC as an evidence-gated compiler and review instrument for
normative text-to-logic systems. Its central design choice is to preserve the
boundary between what can be checked inside a bounded executable subset and
what still requires source evidence, expert annotation, or independent
execution. The implementation supplies that boundary with typed IR, total
fail-closed lowering, provenance packets, diagnostic proof records, and a
review workbench. The current repository supports reproducible engineering
checks and exposes its exploratory and missing measurements, including a
substantial replay mismatch. It does not yet justify a quality or legal-
correctness claim. Completing the declared gates will turn this transparent
instrument into a scientific evaluation; failing a gate will remain a useful,
publishable audit rather than an invisible limitation.
