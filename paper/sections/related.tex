\section{Related work}

\textbf{Semantic parsing and executable evaluation.}
Semantic parsing maps language to machine-interpretable meaning
representations. Spider popularized cross-domain text-to-SQL evaluation
\citep{yu2018spider}, while UNIMER showed that meaning representation, program
aliases, and execution-engine availability materially affect comparisons
\citep{guo2020unimer}. We inherit execution-based evaluation but add explicit
source-evidence and refusal accounting for normative artifacts.

\textbf{Legal language benchmarks.}
CUAD provides expert clause annotations; ContractNLI combines document-level
inference with evidence spans; LegalBench spans multiple types of legal
reasoning; and RuleArena stresses real-world rule selection and application
\citep{hendrycks2021cuad,koreeda2021contractnli,guha2023legalbench,zhou2025rulearena}.
These resources motivate expert anchors and construct stratification, but are
not interchangeable with executable-program equivalence.

\textbf{Computational law and autoformalization.}
Catala demonstrates a language designed for collaborative, executable
formalization of computational law \citep{merigoux2021catala}. Graus provides
a rare source-to-production-DMN benchmark and shows that structural and outcome
similarity are complementary \citep{graus2026legaltext}. Grammars of Formal
Uncertainty studies selective trust in LLM-generated formal artifacts
\citep{ganguly2025grammars}; Wang et al. identify gaps between legal benchmark
accuracy and faithful solver use \citep{wang2026limits}. \lexec{} does not claim
novelty for executable law or solver use. Its contribution is the evidence
contract that keeps source fidelity, internal validity, independent execution,
and operational status from collapsing into one score.

\textbf{Behavioral and selective evaluation.}
CheckList argues for capability-specific behavioral tests beyond aggregate
accuracy \citep{ribeiro2020checklist}, and selective classification separates
coverage from risk \citep{geifman2017selective}. Our protocol combines these
ideas at an artifact boundary: metamorphic and solver checks diagnose declared
behaviors, while refusals remain in a coverage denominator and cannot be used
to inflate conditional quality.
