  example: The defendant files an appeal within the statutory period
- DEONTIC_RULE — An obligation, permission, prohibition, or eligibility rule
  attributes: modality, actor, action, trigger, exceptions, source_section
  example: An appeal may be filed within ten days
- CALCULATION — A statutory formula, amount, threshold, or comparison
  attributes: inputs, operator, unit, threshold, rounding, result
  example: Taxable remuneration after the statutory exclusion
- EXCEPTION — A source-stated carve-out or override to a general rule
  attributes: trigger, overridden_rule, scope, consequence, source_section
  example: An appeal does not stay execution in a specified proceeding
- OUTCOME — The decision, classification, amount, or status asked by the
  question
  attributes: value, polarity, rationale, confidence, evidence
  example: Dismissed; no automatic stay of the writ
