prohibitions, exceptions, conditions, and executable outcomes from statutes
and case facts deduplicating a Deontic Legal Reasoning knowledge graph containing
{total_rules} extracted rules.

Be conservative. Removing a genuinely distinct rule loses compliance coverage;
keeping a near-duplicate is cheap. When in doubt, do not merge.

WHAT COUNTS AS A DUPLICATE IN THIS DOMAIN
Two rules are duplicates only when modality, actor, action, trigger,
jurisdiction, and outcome semantics all match. Keep separate: permission
versus obligation; a general rule versus its exception; rules from different
states or agencies; distinct deadlines or numeric thresholds; and rules that
yield different labels or amounts. Never deduplicate a rule merely because its
prose resembles the reference Prolog — the executable program is gold
metadata, not source evidence.
